Julfar Heritage Corridor
Part of a multi-agency working group comprising private developers, the Department of Antiquities, RAK Tourism Development Authority, and Ras Al Khaimah Municipality to develop the Julfar Heritage Corridor, aimed at enhancing cultural tourism in Ras Al Khaimah. Contributed to the revitalization of Old Ras Al Khaimah as a tourism destination through the proposal of a heritage loop linking key historic sites and attractions, supported by public realm improvement strategies; and prepared design concepts for archaeological heritage sites.
Confidential Project 01
Prepared the concept design for a waterfront residential neighborhood through the transformation of former agricultural plots into a planned residential community. Coordinated closely with the hydrology team to integrate flood mitigation strategies, including channeling and management of floodwater flows to protect the development site.
Confidential Project 02
Developed the concept master plan for the conversion of underutilized agricultural land into residential communities, coordinating closely with hydrology engineers to integrate proposed drainage channels and flood management requirements into the planning framework. Applied GIS-based spatial and topographic analysis to identify suitable development areas within sloping terrain, supporting site-responsive planning, infrastructure coordination, and efficient land utilization.
Confidential Project 03
Prepared the concept master plan for an infill residential development utilizing newly available land created between a proposed hydrology channel and an existing neighborhood. The planning approach focused on integrating the new community with surrounding development while responding to site constraints, infrastructure coordination, and flood management requirements.
Confidential Project 04
Produced master plan for a residential community adjacent to protected mangrove areas, integrating proposed drainage channels as landscape and open space features within the overall community structure. The plan incorporated a continuous sikka network to enhance pedestrian connectivity across the site while preserving key view corridors toward the mangrove landscape, supporting environmentally responsive and community-oriented urban design.
RAK Academic City
Prepared the concept master plan for a proposed Academic City, coordinating closely with RAKEZ as the property owner through multiple presentation and review stages to refine the overall development vision. The master plan organized academic facilities, student and staff residential clusters, and mixed-use activity nodes within walkable distances, promoting a connected and pedestrian-oriented campus environment. A green pedestrian network linked key destinations across the site, with landscaped activity nodes positioned at major intersections to encourage social interaction and enhance the public realm.
Public Realm Studies 01
Prepared a public realm and access study for an existing unplanned settlement, aimed at improving connectivity and ensuring vehicular access to all plots within the community. Developed a shared pedestrian street network integrated with landscaped nodes and small public plazas where space permitted, enhancing walkability, accessibility, and overall public realm quality within the constrained urban fabric.
